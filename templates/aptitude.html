12. You design a groundbreaking AI tool that automates public food distribution, but a government department takes six months just to review the initial paperwork. What do you do?

13. A public-facing e-governance app you manage experiences a server breakdown on a national holiday, causing widespread public complaints online. How do you lead your team?

14. You are building an international data-sharing platform, but a sudden change in national cross-border data privacy regulations makes your current architecture illegal. What is your move?

15. A highly capable teammate is suddenly falling behind on their deliverables, threatening your joint project deadline. What is your approach?

17. Your company transitions a core legacy database system to a brand-new cloud framework you have never heard of before. You need to use it starting next week. How do you adapt?

18. You have five distinct ways to optimize a webpage layout. You have spent two weeks reading comparison articles but still haven't written a single line of code. What is happening?

19. A senior designer reviews your user interface prototype and calls your favorite user journey map "overly complicated and confusing for users." How do you process this?

20. Describe a time you built something (a project, code, artwork, or event) that failed completely when presented. What did you do next, and what did you learn?
